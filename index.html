Introduction.

I've been coding since 2020. I started on university projects in Python, Scala, C++ and C, which gave me a strong foundation in programming fundamentals and problem solving. From there I moved into data science, machine learning and data engineering: optimization, data pipelines, and the craft of building data products that meet real business needs.

Today I'm a Data Science Specialist at CloudWalk, the fintech behind InfinitePay, JIM and Pierre Finance. I'm building the next generation of financial models: a tokenizer for financial events in Rust, and transformer models pre-trained and fine-tuned on GPU clusters. Before that I co-created the model registry, serving and inference platform that scaled our credit infrastructure from 1 to 20+ models, scoring more than 7 million users.

Previously I spent three years at Nubank, across the Credit and Platform teams, contributing to projects that reached more than 100 million customers. I'm also pursuing a Master's in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin.
